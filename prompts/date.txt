The date function is used to convert input values into a standardized date object.



Examples

/*
col : id | room_type | last_review_time | reviews_per_month
row 1 : 2384 | Private room | 11:12 am | 2.92 
row 2 : 15365 | Entire home/apt | 08:20 am | 0.81
row 3 : 17928 | Entire home/apt| 12:12 pm |  2.81
*/
Purpose: Figure out the earliest last review.
Current Operation Purpose: The dataset has accuracy concerns, including date formatting issues in column "last_review_time", which prevent it from meeting all data quality criteria.
Expression: ```value.toDate()```
Explanation: value.toDate("hh:mm a").toString("hh:mm a") convert all the time-like (only hours: hh, and minutes: mm, AM/PM marker) cell values into "hh:mm a" format, h:Hour in AM/PM, m: Minute in hour, a: AM/PM marker .
Output: 11:12 AM| 08:20 AM | 12:12 PM
