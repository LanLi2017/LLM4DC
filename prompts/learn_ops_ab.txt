row 6: | 100000    | Chicagoo  | IL    |  
*/

Selected Operation: ```{"columnName": "City", "operation":"mass_edit", args: "[{'from': ['Hon', 'HONOLULU', 'Honolulu'], 'to': 'Honolulu'}, {'from': ['CHI', 'Chicagoo'], 'to': 'Chicago'}]"}```
Explanation: operation mass_edit is applied to column City, with args as a list of dictionaries(replace cell values in 'from' with 'to'): standardize different representations (Hon, HONOLULU, Honolulu) to Honolulu; Similarly, CHI and Chicagoo are repaired to Chicago.
