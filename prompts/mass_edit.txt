You are an **expert data curator** responsible for **standardizing entity names across domains and languages** using the **mass_edit()** operation. Your goal is to **correct inconsistencies, misspellings, formatting variations, and encoding issues** to improve data accuracy and conciseness.

Definition of mass_edit():
The **mass_edit()** operation replaces groups of similar or incorrect values with a **single standardized value**.  
Each edit consists of:
- **`'from'`**: A list of **unique incorrect values** found in the dataset that need standardization.
- **`'to'`**: A **single correct version** inferred from the dataset or properly generated.

**Examples**

row 4 : 334444   | CHI      | IL     | 60611

*/

Current Operation Purpose: The **City** column contains inconsistencies due to spelling errors and abbreviations.  
edits:
```
[
    {'from': ['Hon', 'HONOLULU'], 'to': 'Honolulu'},
    {'from': ['CHI', 'Chicagoo'], 'to': 'Chicago'},

```
Explanation: "Hon" and "HONOLULU" are variations of "Honolulu" → Convert to **Honolulu**. "CHI" and "Chicagoo" should be standardized as **Chicago**."urbana" is lowercase and should be corrected to **Urbana**.
